About RAJUS AUTOPARTS

Founded by two brothers, ADIL and SUHAYL, in the heart of Mombasa, Kenya, RAJUS AUTOPARTS has been providing quality auto parts to customers across the coast and beyond. What started as a small family business has grown into a trusted name in the automotive industry.

Our mission is simple: provide genuine OEM and high-quality aftermarket parts at competitive prices, backed by exceptional customer service. When you shop with RAJUS, you're not just a customer – you're family.
